Report a missing animal

Help reveal where animals are going missing across Aotearoa. Every report becomes a pin — and when reports cluster in an area, patterns and hotspots become visible. Your report shows an approximate area only, and is reviewed before it appears.

Before you submit

  • Report the general area — suburb and region. An exact street number isn't needed.
  • This map is for spotting patterns, not active searches — for that, dedicated lost-pet pages can help too.
  • A rough date and the suburb are the most useful details for revealing clusters.
  • Reports are reviewed before publishing.

Found an animal?

The quickest way home is a microchip scan — and it's free. Take the animal to any vet clinic, SPCA centre, or animal shelter; they can scan for a microchip on the spot and contact the registered owner. We don't hold owner details ourselves — a quick scan is the safest, fastest way to get an animal home.
